## Repair under test

`browsergym_set_of_marks="1"`. The adapter now recognizes that runtime marker
and presents a normal bounded `activate` affordance, which binds to the
existing bid-based typed click action. No task name, selector, or coordinate is
authored by the planner.

The earlier zero-affordance failures for both navigation tasks did not recur.
Two `navigate-tree` episodes still reached an official reward of zero after
two click steps; those are retained as `EXECUTION` evidence. SVG grid and
drag controls remain outside this repair and require the planned visual/gesture
binder rather than a prompt change.
